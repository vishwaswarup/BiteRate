      How it works

      A small, transparent model

      BiteRate is trained on {{ '{:,}'.format(model_info.train_size) if model_info else 'about ten thousand' }} labelled restaurant reviews from two sources: short English reviews and longer, star-rated reviews from Zomato.

      1. 01

        Clean

        Contractions are expanded and words lemmatised. Negations are kept and attached to the words they modify, so “not good” is never read as “good”.

      2. 02

        Represent

        Each review becomes word and character n-gram features, which also copes with typos, slang and emoji.

      3. 03

        Classify

        A logistic regression scores each class. Because it is linear, every prediction can be traced back to the words that drove it.
